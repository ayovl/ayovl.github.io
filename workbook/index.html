WEEK ONE · IMPOSSIBLE SURVIVORS · DAY 1

She Survived the Highest Fall in History

SAY IT LIKE THISA flight attendant once fell six miles out of an exploded plane with no parachute, and she lived to talk about it.

Vesna Vulovic was 22 and working as a flight attendant on JAT Flight 367, flying from Stockholm to Belgrade on January 26, 1972. Forty-six minutes after takeoff from Copenhagen, a bomb hidden in the baggage hold exploded and tore the DC-9 apart at 10,160 meters over Czechoslovakia. Twenty-seven of the twenty-eight people aboard died. Vulovic, trapped in the tail section and pinned by a food cart, fell the entire way inside the wreckage. A former medic named Bruno Honke found her screaming in the snow-covered wreckage and kept her alive until help arrived. She had a fractured skull, three broken vertebrae, a broken pelvis and two broken legs, and she stayed in a coma for twenty-seven days. Her survival is still the highest fall anyone has lived through without a parachute.

ONE MORE THINGWhen Guinness honored her in 1985, Paul McCartney personally presented her record certificate at a ceremony in London.
